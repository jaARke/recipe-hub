Peach Cobbler with Biscuit Topping

Published August 9, 2026 / No comments
Jump to Recipe · Print Recipe

Late summer peaches do not need much help. This cobbler keeps the fruit simple and puts all the attention on a soft, cakey biscuit lid that browns in the oven while the juices bubble up around it.

Serve it warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream, or cold from the fridge the next morning if any survives the night.

Peach cobbler with biscuit topping

  • Author: Aunt June
  • Prep Time: 20 minutes
  • Cook Time: 45 minutes
  • Yield: 8 servings
  • Category: Dessert
  • Method: Baking
Print Recipe
Pin Recipe

Description

Ripe peaches bubbling under a tender, sugar-dusted biscuit topping. June made it all summer long.

Ingredients

Scale

For the filling

  • 6 ripe peaches, sliced
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch

For the biscuit topping

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 6 tablespoons cold butter, cubed
  • ¾ cup cream

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Toss the peaches with the sugar and cornstarch and spread them in a baking dish.
  2. Whisk the flour and baking powder, then rub in the butter until crumbly.
  3. Stir in the cream until a soft dough forms and drop spoonfuls over the peaches.
  4. Bake 40 to 45 minutes until the topping is golden and the fruit bubbles at the edges.

Dinner

Lemon garlic roast chicken with potatoes

A whole chicken rubbed with lemon, garlic, and thyme, roasted over a bed of little potatoes that catch every drip.

Recipe from Aunt June

Serves
4
Prep
15 min
Cook
1 hr 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, about 4 pounds
  • 2 lemons
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1½ pounds small potatoes, halved
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon fresh thyme

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F and pat the chicken very dry.
  2. Mash four garlic cloves with the thyme, the zest of one lemon, the oil, and a generous pinch of salt. Rub it all over the chicken.
  3. Toss the potatoes in a roasting pan with the remaining garlic and a little oil. Set the chicken on top with a squeezed lemon half inside.
  4. Roast 70 to 80 minutes, until the juices run clear and the potatoes are browned.
  5. Rest the chicken 10 minutes, then squeeze the other lemon over everything.

Cook's notes

Aunt June tucked the squeezed lemon halves inside the bird and swore it kept the breast juicy. She served it with plenty of crusty bread for the pan juices.
